01 — Information collection and use

We don't collect anything

We don't collect, store, log, track, or share any personal data, usage statistics, or device identifiers, ever.

02 — Third-party services

No SDKs riding along

Our applications don't include third-party analytics tools, advertising networks, or software development kits that collect your information. What you install is what runs — nothing quietly reporting home in the background.

03 — Children's privacy

Safe by design, for every age

Because the Service doesn't collect personal information from anyone, that includes children under 13. There's no data pipeline that could pick up a child's information, knowingly or otherwise.

In short: if we collect nothing from everyone, we collect nothing from kids either.
